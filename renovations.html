Featured · Decking
Lakefront Deck Transformation · Stevensville, MI

Premium decking, before & after.

When this Lake Michigan homeowner wanted to bring their tired pressure-treated deck up to modern standards, they came to Captain's Choice. We tore out the old wood railing, reframed the structure to support a covered second-story extension with bay windows, wrapped the lower level in a cantilevered window-wall to capture every angle of the lake, and topped the whole thing with composite decking and stainless cable-rail.

This is the same composite-and-cable system we install on lakefront homes from St. Joseph and Bridgman to New Buffalo and South Haven — engineered to survive Michigan winters without warping, splintering, or fading. Built for hosting in July. Built for the next thirty Julys.

Featured · Historic Restoration
Historic Restoration · St. Joseph, MI

The Library Historic Hotel.

In 2024, Shannon Kutchek and John Kenna acquired the original 1903 Andrew Carnegie library on Main Street with one ambition: bring it back to life as a boutique hotel. They needed a general contractor who could honor 120 years of history while meeting modern hospitality standards. They chose Captain's Choice.

Across the project, our team has handled structural carpentry, custom millwork, period-appropriate finish work, and the build-out of the basement Archive Lounge speakeasy — the kind of meticulous detail you only see when the same crew sees a build through from foundation to final walk-through.

The Library Historic Hotel is now one of Southwest Michigan's most acclaimed boutique stays — and we're proud to be the contractor on it.

Featured · New Construction
Custom Barndominium · Berrien County, MI

Modern barndominium build.

Barndominiums have become one of the most-requested new builds across Southwest Michigan — wide open floor plans, dramatic timber-frame entries, and the durability of metal exteriors. Captain's Choice handles them ground up: site prep, foundation, steel framing, timber-frame architectural details, electrical, mechanical rough-ins, and full interior buildout.

This Berrien County build features a black standing-seam metal exterior, an exposed timber-frame covered entry, and a flexible interior plan ready for residential, workshop, or hospitality use. We can do it for you across Berrien, Cass, Van Buren, and St. Joseph counties — and into Northwest Indiana.
